## 3. Transit Damage vs Operational Defect
1. **Visible Transit Damage**: If the appliance exterior exhibits dents, shattered glass, or bent framing upon delivery, the customer must upload photographs within 48 hours of delivery. A direct replacement unit is prioritized.
2. **Missing Components**: If installation kits, inlet/outlet hoses, remote controls, or stabilizer cables are missing, the seller dispatches an expedited component kit rather than returning the entire appliance.
3. **Post-Installation Functional Failure**: If an appliance fails during initial testing by the installation engineer, a Technician Inspection Report (TIR) certifying DOA (Dead On Arrival) is submitted to approve immediate replacement.
