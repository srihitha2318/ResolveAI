## 1. Scope & Applicable Products
This category policy applies to all consumer electronics, smartphones, tablets, laptops, audio gear, computing accessories, and wearable technology sold on the ResolveAI marketplace platform in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 2. Return & Replacement Window
1. **7-Calendar-Day Replacement Window**:
   - Customers may report manufacturing defects, dead-on-arrival (DOA) hardware, transit damage, or wrong items within **7 calendar days** of confirmed delivery (`delivered_at`).
   - Replacement with an identical model is the primary remedy. Monetary refunds are evaluated only if replacement inventory is unavailable.
2. **Post 7-Day Window**: Inquiries received after 7 days fall under the manufacturer warranty and must be directed to authorized brand service centers across India.

## 3. Required Evidence for Claims
- **Photographic / Video Documentation**: Clear photos of the outer box with shipping label, device serial number or IMEI barcode, and the defect area.
- **Serial Number / IMEI Verification**: The device serial number captured during unboxing must match the dispatch scan recorded in the seller dispatch manifest.
- **Packaging Condition**: Original manufacturer retail box, bundled chargers, cables, SIM ejector tools, and manuals must be retained.

## 4. Decision Rules & Inspection
1. **Dead On Arrival (DOA) / Functional Failure**:
   - For items valued up to ₹5,000.00 (5,00,000 paise), automated agent assessment can approve replacement upon visual diagnostic verification.
   - For items exceeding ₹5,000.00 up to ₹25,000.00, remote diagnostic check or brand service center DOA certificate is verified.
   - For high-value laptops/smartphones exceeding ₹25,000.00 (25,00,000 paise), an authorized technician doorstep verification or Tier 2 supervisor approval is required prior to replacement dispatch.
2. **Missing In-Box Accessories**: If accessories (e.g. charging cable) are missing but main device is intact, a partial accessory reshipment is issued rather than returning the entire device.

## 5. Exclusions
- Devices with physical liquid ingress, cracked screens post-setup without transit damage evidence, unauthorized bootloader unlocking, or third-party repair attempts are strictly excluded from return.
