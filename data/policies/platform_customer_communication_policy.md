## 1. Principles of Customer Engagement
This policy guides automated response agents and customer care representatives on customer communication standards across the ResolveAI marketplace platform. Communications must reflect empathy, clarity, honesty, and cultural sensitivity.

## 2. Core Communication Standards
1. **Clear & Jargon-Free Language**: Avoid internal logistical or technical codes (e.g., avoid "RTO SLA breach NDR"; state clearly "Your package could not be delivered and is being returned to our warehouse").
2. **Transparent Uncertainty**: When factual records are incomplete (such as inactive carrier tracking or unverified defect photos), agents must state the uncertainty honestly rather than fabricating timelines.
3. **Strict Prohibition of False Promises**:
   - Never promise that a refund or replacement has been processed or completed without verified backend ledger entries.
   - Never guarantee a specific hour of courier arrival unless verified by carrier dispatch telemetry.
4. **Respectful & Empathetic Demeanor**: Acknowledge customer inconvenience respectfully regardless of dispute severity. Never accuse customers of dishonesty or misrepresentation.

## 3. Communication Formats in Indian Context
- Amounts must always be clearly stated in Indian Rupees using proper notation (e.g., "₹18,999.00", not "18999" or generic symbols).
- Timeframes must reference standard Indian working hours and Indian Standard Time (IST).

## 4. Worked Communication Examples
- **Unacceptable**: *"Your refund of 5000 has been sent to your bank account."* (Violates truthfulness if no PG gateway record exists).
- **Compliant**: *"We have approved your refund request of ₹5,000.00 in our internal records. The transfer is currently awaiting clearing by our payment processing partner and typically reflects in your original UPI/bank account within 3 to 5 business days."*
- **Compliant Delayed Shipment Update**: *"We observe that your order #ORD-2026-003 is currently delayed at the regional logistics hub in Hyderabad. We have raised an urgent carrier inquiry and will update you with the confirmed delivery status within 24 hours."*
